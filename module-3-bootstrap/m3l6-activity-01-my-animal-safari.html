Forest

Dense forests are home to deer, foxes, owls, and countless insect species that thrive beneath the canopy. The layered structure of trees, shrubs, and ground cover supports a huge variety of life.

Ocean

The ocean hosts everything from tiny plankton to massive whales. Coral reefs alone support roughly a quarter of all known marine species, making them some of the richest ecosystems on Earth.

Desert

Despite harsh conditions, deserts support camels, scorpions, and reptiles that have adapted to survive extreme heat and scarce water over millions of years.
