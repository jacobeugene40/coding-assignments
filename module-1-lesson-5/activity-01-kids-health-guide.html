Healthy Plate

Balanced meals give your body the fuel it needs to grow, focus, and play. Try filling half your plate with fruits and vegetables at every meal.

Learn more about building a balanced plate

Not all snacks are created equal. Choosing whole foods over sugary treats helps keep your energy steady throughout the day.

Nutrition Tips

Drink More Water

Water helps your body work properly and keeps you feeling alert. Try carrying a water bottle with you during the day.

Read more nutrition tips

Eat a Rainbow

Different colored fruits and vegetables give your body different vitamins. Try to eat at least three colors at every meal.
