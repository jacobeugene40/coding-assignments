Planet Earth — Quick Guide

A quick guide to our planet, covering how to protect it, where to marvel at it, and how to talk about it.

Five Steps to Protect the Planet

  1. Reduce single-use plastic in your daily routine
  2. Conserve water by fixing leaks and taking shorter showers
  3. Recycle paper, glass, and metal whenever possible
  4. Plant trees or support local reforestation efforts
  5. Choose walking, cycling, or public transport over driving when you can

Incredible Natural Wonders

  • The Grand Canyon, USA
  • The Great Barrier Reef, Australia
  • Mount Everest, Nepal/China border
  • The Amazon Rainforest, South America

Key Environmental Terms

Biodiversity
The variety of plant and animal life in a particular habitat or in the world as a whole.
Climate Change
Long-term shifts in temperatures and weather patterns, largely driven by human activity since the 1800s.
Sustainability
Meeting present needs without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own needs.
