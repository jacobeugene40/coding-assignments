Inline Quotes

Marie Curie once said nothing in life is to be feared, it is only to be understood, reminding us that curiosity conquers fear.

When asked about failure, Thomas Edison famously replied that he had not failed, he had just found ten thousand ways that did not work.

The poet Rumi wrote that the wound is the place where the light enters you, a line still quoted by readers today.
