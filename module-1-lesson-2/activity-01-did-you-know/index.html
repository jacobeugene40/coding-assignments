Did You Know?

Welcome to a page full of surprising facts about our world. Scroll down to explore categories, ranked from the biggest topics to the smallest details.

Space Facts

A day on Venus is longer than a year on Venus. The planet takes 243 Earth days to rotate once, but only 225 Earth days to orbit the Sun.

Our Solar System

Jupiter has at least 95 known moons, more than any other planet in our solar system.

Fun Star Fact

Neutron stars are so dense that a teaspoon of their material would weigh about a billion tons on Earth.

Animal Facts

Octopuses have three hearts and blue blood, which helps them survive in the cold, low-oxygen depths of the ocean.

Tiny Creatures

A single teaspoon of soil can contain up to a billion bacteria and thousands of other microorganisms.

Bird Fact

The Arctic Tern migrates from the Arctic to the Antarctic and back every year, covering roughly 44,000 miles.

Quick Trivia

Honey never spoils. Archaeologists have found pots of honey in ancient Egyptian tombs that are still edible after 3,000 years.

One Last Thing

Bananas are berries, but strawberries are not, according to the botanical definition of a berry.
