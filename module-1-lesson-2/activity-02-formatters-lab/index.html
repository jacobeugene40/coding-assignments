Chemistry

Water is made of two hydrogen atoms and one oxygen atom, written as H2O.

The chemical symbol for gold, Au, comes from its Latin name, aurum.

Table salt is sodium chloride, a compound essential to human health in small amounts.

Physics

Einstein's famous equation is written as E = mc2, linking energy and mass.

The speed of light is approximately 299,792 kilometers per second, an almost unimaginable pace.

It was once believed that atoms are the smallest unit of matter, but this was disproven with the discovery of subatomic particles.

Biology

The human body contains around 37 trillion cells, each with its own specialized function.

DNA stands for deoxyribonucleic acid, the molecule that carries genetic instructions.

Adults have 206 bones, while babies are born with about 300, many of which fuse together over time.

Early scientists believed the human body had only five senses, though many researchers now argue there are more.

Fast Facts

Fun fact: the mitochondria is often called the powerhouse of the cell.

Carbon dioxide is written chemically as CO2, made of one carbon atom and two oxygen atoms.
