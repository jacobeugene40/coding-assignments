Snow Leopard

Scientific Name: Panthera uncia

Habitat: Mountain ranges of Central and South Asia, at elevations up to 5,500 meters.

Diet: Carnivore — feeds mainly on blue sheep, ibex, and other mountain-dwelling animals.

Fun Fact: A snow leopard's tail can be almost as long as its body, helping it balance on steep, rocky terrain.

Conservation Status: Vulnerable, with an estimated population of fewer than 10,000 adults left in the wild.
