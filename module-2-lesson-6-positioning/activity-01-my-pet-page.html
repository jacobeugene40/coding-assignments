Here are a few of Buddy's favorite moments, captured over the past year of adventures.

Buddy loves the park on sunny mornings, chasing squirrels he never quite catches.

Every evening, Buddy waits by the door for his walk, tail wagging with excitement.

His favorite trick is "shake," which he performs perfectly for a treat.

Buddy's best friend is the neighbor's cat, Whiskers, despite what you might expect.

On rainy days, Buddy prefers to curl up on the couch with his favorite blanket.

He's not a fan of baths, but loves the towel-drying afterward.

Buddy has learned to "sit," "stay," "shake," and "roll over" — his newest trick.

Every year on his birthday, Buddy gets a special peanut butter cake made just for him.
